# Editable manuscript figures

These are original analytical diagrams, drawn directly in TikZ and compiled by the main manuscript. They are not screenshots, benchmark charts, or copies of a prior paper's figures.

- [Architecture](architecture.tex): information and authority boundaries of selected implementation paths.
- [Recovery sequence](recovery.tex): hypothetical lost acknowledgement and stale-owner rejection; not an observed experiment.
- [Evidence relations](evidence.tex): exact source, complete read, explicit selection, publication, and independent acceptance.

The earlier foundation [figure inputs](../../figures/README.md) remain separate historical inputs and were not modified. Figure numbers and captions are defined only in the manuscript chapters.
